# DISCRIPTION

This is a game made during jmc gamedev hackathon .
Ihis game is dual and single player ,
**TURN BASED GAME** --> 
Players take turns to play ,
once at a time,
one payer chooses to mark any of the box in a 3x3 grid ,
with it's own symbol ,

to win this game one have to align three symbols in a line
straight , vertical , horizontal or cross sectional .

# POSSIBILITIES
1. **ONE PLAYER WINS** -->
   when either of the one satisfies the wining conditions ,
   that are to align their symbol in a striaght line ,
   either vertical , horizontal or cross sectional in a
   3x3 grid .
2. **DRAW** -->
   this happens when all the boxes in a
   3x3 grid are filled with either of players'
   symbols and none of them satisfies the wining
   conditions that are aligning three symbols
   in a straight line either in vertical , horizontal
   or cross sectional in a 3x3 grid.
