# DISCRIPTION

This is a game made during jmc gamedev hackathon .\n
Ihis game is dual and single player ,\n
**TURN BASED GAME** --> \n
Players take turns to play ,\n
once at a time,\n
one payer chooses to mark any of the box in a 3x3 grid ,\n
with it's own symbol ,\n

to win this game one have to align three symbols in a line\n
straight , vertical , horizontal or cross sectional .\n

# POSSIBILITIES
1. **ONE PLAYER WINS** -->\n
   when either of the one satisfies the wining conditions ,\n
   that are to align their symbol in a striaght line ,\n
   either vertical , horizontal or cross sectional in a\n
   3x3 grid .\n
2. **DRAW** -->\n
   this happens when all the boxes in a\n
   3x3 grid are filled with either of players'\n
   symbols and none of them satisfies the wining\n
   conditions that are aligning three symbols\n
   in a straight line either in vertical , horizontal\n
   or cross sectional in a 3x3 grid.\n
